Overview & objectives

This project analyzes how the events of October 7, 2023 affected three things in and around Israel: internet traffic, device usage (desktop vs. mobile), and cyberattack activity — specifically network-layer (L3) attacks. The goal is to make a complex, sensitive subject legible to a general-public audience.

Every design decision serves that audience: we use only familiar chart types (lines, areas, bars, choropleth maps), plain-language labels, and small ⓘ info-buttons that explain technical terms in context — for example, what an “L3 attack” is — so a non-technical reader can follow the story without a glossary.

Audience

General public — no networking or security background assumed.

Three lenses

Traffic · device usage · L3 cyberattacks, all tied to Oct 7 2023.

Geographic focus

Israel, contextualized against world-wide baselines.

Time span

Oct 7 2023 crisis window, plus L3 trends across 2023–2025.

Data sourcing & preparation

All data comes from Cloudflare Radar, exported as JSON and CSV. Cloudflare’s public API restricts raw byte-volume figures, so the dashboard relies only on relative / normalized measures — percentages and indexed values rather than absolute traffic in bytes.

Pipeline

  • Source: Cloudflare Radar exports (traffic, device split, and L3 attack feeds).
  • Restriction handled: raw byte volumes were unavailable, so we use normalized %/indexed measures throughout — which also makes Israel-vs-World comparisons fair.
  • Cleaning & reshaping in Python: we cleaned the exports and reshaped them from wide → long form so each metric/time/region became a tidy row, ready for Tableau.
Because only relative measures were available, the design deliberately frames every chart around change and proportion (spikes, shares, %) rather than absolute magnitude.

Core visualizations

Three coordinated dashboards, each answering one question.

① Traffic Volume & Device Usage

  • Area chart of Oct 7 traffic over the 48-hour crisis window.
  • Desktop-vs-mobile multi-line comparison — Israel vs. World.
  • Dual-line general-traffic view.

② Network L3 attacks (2023 & 2024)

  • Choropleth map of global attack origins (where attacks come from).
  • Time-series of attack volume with interactive range sliders.

③ Israel layer-attack & industry targets (2023–2025)

  • Multi-line trends of normalized L3 attack traffic.
  • Grouped bars of attack share.
  • Color-coded heatmap of which industries are targeted.

Design strategies

Structured around Shneiderman’s mantra — overview first, zoom & filter, details on demand — with coordinated multiple views and cross-filtering. We applied the four classic operators: Derive · Manipulate · Facet · Reduce.

Derive

Compute new values from the source: raw feeds → normalized percentages and indexed measures, so different regions and metrics are comparable.

Manipulate

Interactive controls: time sliders, map pan/zoom, and linked filters that cross-filter the coordinated views.

Facet

Partition the data into views: juxtaposition (side-by-side small multiples) and superimposition (overlaid lines on shared axes).

Reduce

Manage clutter: aggregate to hourly / weekly buckets and show only the Top-N industries instead of the long tail.

Shneiderman’s mantra, applied

  • Overview first — the landing dashboard summarizes traffic, device split and attacks at a glance.
  • Zoom & filter — sliders, map pan and linked filters let readers drill into a window or region.
  • Details on demand — tooltips and ⓘ info-buttons surface definitions and exact values only when asked for.
  • Coordinated multiple views — selecting in one chart cross-filters the others.

Key findings

Sharp Oct 7 traffic spikes. Israeli internet traffic spiked sharply at ~03:30 and again at ~16:00 on October 7, 2023 — visible as twin peaks in the area chart.
IT is the most-targeted sector. From Oct 2023 through Dec 2024, Information Technology was consistently the most-targeted industry — often accounting for more than half of L3 attacks against Israel.
Attacks track geopolitics. Attack spikes correlate with geopolitical events: a surge as ground operations ramped up in late October 2023, and a “copycat” spike around the October 2024 anniversary.
