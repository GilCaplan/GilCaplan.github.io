Steer to Feel

Steering abstract concepts into Vision-Language Models — do images act as steering signals?

Gil Caplan & Anis Barhoum  ·  Adversarial ML 02360207
Qwen2-VL 2B / 7B Llama-3.2-Vision 11B activation steering emotion + weather concepts inference-time, no fine-tuning

The question

Activation steering is well known for text LLMs: add a direction vector to the hidden states and push the model toward a concept. This project asks whether the same works for the visual / multimodal pathway of a Vision-Language Model — can an image-derived steering vector inject an abstract concept (an emotion or a weather mood) into the model's output without changing the input pixels or the model weights?

The method computes a batch-averaged steering vector as mean(activations on concept images) − mean(activations on neutral images), then injects it at a chosen transformer layer during generation, scaled by a strength α. Two model components are probed independently: the LLM (language) layers and the Vision encoder layers.

Two concept domains

  • Emotion — FER faces dataset: happy, sad, neutral, angry
  • Weather — multiclass weather dataset: cloudy, rain, shine, sunrise

Both datasets are pulled automatically via kagglehub on first run (see Load_dataset.py).

Two experiments

  • Controlled Hallucination — inject a concept while the model looks at a blank noise image; does the concept appear in the description from nothing?
  • Semantic Transfer — show a real image of concept A, steer toward concept B (e.g. happy→sad, shine→rain); does the description flip?

Findings (real figures from the repo)

Effectiveness is measured as a keyword-match rate: does the steered description contain concept keywords that the unsteered baseline does not. Results are reported per model, per concept, and swept across layers and α.

What the data shows

  • Image-derived vectors do steer. A vector built purely from concept vs. neutral images can inject that concept into the text output — even onto a blank noise image (controlled hallucination).
  • Layer and component matter. Effectiveness is not uniform: it varies strongly with injection depth and differs between the LLM and Vision encoder pathways.
  • Model scale helps cleanliness. Llama-3.2-11B yields the clearest signal (baseline≈0, positive lift) in the tightened keyword evaluation.
